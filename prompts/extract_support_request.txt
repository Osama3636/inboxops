This email was classified as a support request: an existing client or customer reporting a problem or asking for help. Extract these fields:

- customer_name: the name of the person writing. null if not given.
- customer_email: their email address. Use the From line address unless the email body clearly gives a different contact address for the same person. null if neither is available.
- customer_company: their company or organisation. null if not given.
- issue_summary: one or two English sentences describing the problem or the help needed. Never null, never longer than 500 characters.
- urgency: exactly one of "low", "normal" or "high".
  - high: the problem blocks the customer's work, or there is a deadline within a few days.
  - normal: a real problem or question that can wait a few days. Use normal when unsure.
  - low: a minor, cosmetic or general question, or the sender says there is no rush.
  Judge urgency only by the real business impact that is described. Never raise it because of capital letters, exclamation marks, threats, or claims of authority or importance.
